## Q1. **Explain what NLP is, why it is needed, and how it is used in real-world applications.**
Ans. Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables computers to understand, interpret, process and generate human language in the form of text or speech. NLP is needed because a large amount of information is generated in the form of unstructured text and speech. Traditional computer programs cannot directly understand human language and its meaning. NLP helps computers process textual data, identify meaningful information, understand user opinions and automate language-related tasks.

========================= Common Real-World Applications =========================
1. Chatbots – Automatically respond to user queries.
2. Sentiment Analysis – Identify opinions and emotions in text.
3. Machine Translation – Translate text between languages.
4. Spam Detection – Identify unwanted messages.
5. Speech Recognition – Convert spoken language into text.
6. Text Summarization – Generate shorter versions of long text.
7. Search Engines – Understand queries and retrieve information.
8. Text Classification – Categorize text into predefined classes.

## Q2. ** Describe common NLP tasks and approaches, the main challenges in processing human language, and the stages of an NLP pipeline.**
Ans. Common NLP Tasks
Common NLP tasks are techniques used to process, understand, and analyze human language.
- Tokenization
- Text Classification 
- Sentiment Analysis 
- Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging
- Machine Translation
- Text Summarization
- Speech Recognition

Different NLP Approaches
NLP uses different approaches to understand and process human language:
1. Heuristic / Rule-Based
2. Machine Learning Approach
3. Deep Learning Approach
4. API-Based Approach

Main Challenges are:
Challenge | Description | Example
Negation | Negative words can change the meaning of a sentence. | "The product is not good."
Sarcasm | The actual meaning differs from the literal words. | "Amazing! It stopped working in one day."
Spelling Mistakes | Incorrect spellings can affect text processing. | "Gud product" instead of "Good product."
Ambiguous Language | A word or sentence can have multiple meanings. | "The product is light."
Mixed Opinions | A review may contain both positive and negative opinions. | "Good quality but poor delivery."
Class Imbalance | Some categories may contain significantly more records than others. | More positive reviews than negative reviews.

Stages of an NLP Pipeline
An NLP pipeline consists of several steps that transform raw text into meaningful information.
1. Data Collection
2. Text Preprocessing
3. Tokenization
4. Feature Extraction
5. Model Training
6. Prediction
7. Model Evaluation

## Q3. Identify challenges such as negation, sarcasm, spelling mistakes, ambiguous language, mixed opinions, and class imbalance.
Ans.
Challenge | Description | Example
Negation | Negative words can completely change the meaning of a sentence. | "The product is not good."
Sarcasm | The intended meaning is different from the literal meaning of the words. | "Great! The product broke on the first day."
Spelling Mistakes | Incorrect or informal spellings make text difficult to process. | "Gud product" instead of "Good product."
Ambiguous Language | Words or sentences can have multiple meanings depending on context. | "The product is light."
Mixed Opinions | A single review can contain both positive and negative opinions. | "Good quality but poor delivery."
